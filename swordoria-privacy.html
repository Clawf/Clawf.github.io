NEDI Idle RPG — Privacy Policy

Updated September 21, 2026

NEDI Idle RPG: Auto Battler (also known as Swordoria, Android package com.nedigames.swordoria) is a single-player game published under the Nedi Games brand by Clawf.

Game progress

The game does not require an account, login or chat. Your progress is stored in the app on your device, including a native backup copy used to recover from WebView storage loss. We do not receive your saved progress. There is no in-game account-based cloud save or cross-device sync. Android may back up or restore app data depending on your device settings and backup provider.

Advertising and third-party data

The app uses Google AdMob for rewarded and interstitial advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, interactions with the app and ads, and diagnostic information such as performance and crash data. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Ads require an internet connection; the core game can be played offline.

Read Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services for details of Google's processing and retention.

Your privacy choices

Where required, Google's consent form lets you choose how data may be used before the app requests ads. A Privacy options button is available on the game's leagues screen when Google requires a way to revisit those choices. You can also manage or delete the advertising ID through your Android privacy settings. Declining personalised advertising does not prevent you from playing the core game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, please contact us.

Deletion and retention

You can delete the local game progress using Android's app storage controls. Uninstalling removes the app's local data, but your Android backup provider may retain a backup according to its settings. Nedi Games has no game-account database to delete. For data handled by Google, use the controls and contact options in Google's privacy policy. If you email us for support, we receive and retain your email and the information you send as needed to respond to your request.

Contact and updates

For questions or privacy requests, email midjourneysubscriber@gmail.com. We will publish future policy changes on this page.
