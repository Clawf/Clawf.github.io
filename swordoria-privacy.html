Swordoria — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

Swordoria ("the game") is a single-player game published by Clawf. This policy explains what data the game handles.

Data we collect

The game itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no registration, and no chat or social features. Your game progress is saved only on your own device.

Advertising

The game shows advertisements served by Google AdMob. To provide and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
  • IP address and coarse location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This processing is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization in your device settings under Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

All game progress is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side data to delete.
