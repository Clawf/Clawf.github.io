Nedi Stamp — Privacy Policy

Effective 20 September 2026

The short version

Nedi Stamp is a puzzle game. We do not ask you for your name, your email address or any other personal detail. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and we run no server of our own: the game never sends your progress anywhere. It stays on your device.

The game does show ads from Google AdMob, and it sells optional items through Google Play. Those two Google services collect data of their own, and that is described below.

What the game stores on your device

  • Your progress and settings: levels finished, stars, gold, lives, sound and music choices, and which optional items you have bought.
  • It is written to the app's own storage on your device. Android may include it in your own device backup, which is your Google account, not ours.
  • We do not upload it and we cannot read it.

To delete it: clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the game.

What the game does not do

  • No analytics or tracking software of ours. The game contains no analytics, crash-reporting or attribution kit, and the game code makes no network request of its own.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no friends list, no user-made content.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location. The app asks for no runtime permissions at all.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, and we run no advertising of our own.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Ads are supplied by Google AdMob. To choose, show and measure ads, Google may collect and use device information, including the advertising ID, a rough idea of where you are (usually from your IP address), and how you interact with ads. What Google does with it is explained in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's own consent screen appears in the game before any ad is requested, and you choose there whether your data may be used for personalised advertising.

  • If you agree, Google may show personalised ads.
  • If you refuse, the game still shows ads, but Google serves non-personalised ones, which use much less data. Ads switch off completely only in the places where Google tells the game that no ad may be requested at all.
  • You can change your answer at any time: open the settings button in the game and choose Ad privacy choices. That reopens Google's screen.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID for every app, in Android's own settings, under Privacy and then Ads.

Buying Premium removes all ads from the game.

Purchases (Google Play)

The optional items (Premium and gold packs) are sold by Google Play. Google handles the payment and keeps the payment record; we never see your card, your address or your Google account details. The game is told only which items you own, so that it can give them to you and bring them back on a new device when you tap Restore purchases. Google's handling of a purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this game, write to us and we will look into it.

Your choices

  • Change or withdraw your ad consent: in the game, settings, Ad privacy choices.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android settings, Privacy, Ads.
  • Remove everything the game stores: clear the app's data, or uninstall it.
  • Ask us a question about your data: the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date at the top.
